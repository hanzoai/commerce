# Personal Data Breach Notification Procedure

## Scope
This procedure applies in the event of a personal data breach under Article 33 of the
GDPR – Notification of a personal data breach to the supervisory authority – and Article 34
– Communication of a personal data breach to the data subject.

The GDPR draws a distinction between a ‘data controller’ and a ‘data processor’ in order
to recognise that not all organisations involved in the processing of personal data have
the same degree of responsibility. Each organisation should establish whether it is data
controller, or a data processor for the same data processing activity; or whether it is
a joint controller.

## Responsibility
All users, including employees, contractors, third-party users, temporary
employees, and officers of [ORG-NAME] are required to be aware of and act in
accordance with this procedure in the event of a personal data breach. Any known
or suspected personal data breaches must also be reported to the Data Protection
Officer.

## Procedure - Breach notification - data controller to data subject
1. If a personal data breach is likely to result in high risk to the rights and
   freedoms of a data subject, [ORG-NAME] notifies those affected without undue
   delay.
2. The notification shall describe the breach in clear and plain language, and
   also any reporting to supervisory authorities (described above).
3. If the breach affects a high volume of subjects, [ORG-NAME] may elect for a
   public communication in lieu of a private one. In this case, a press release
   will be made available, describing the breach in clear, plain language and any
   reporting to supervisory authorities.
